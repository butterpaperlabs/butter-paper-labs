About this app

A GPS camera that remembers the place for you.

PinCam is a GPS map camera for iPhone and Android. Every photo you take is stamped with a live map, the full address, GPS coordinates, the date and time, and, if you want them, altitude, compass direction and the weather. Then it quietly files the photo by city and area, so you can find it again in seconds.

We built PinCam because a photo of a place is only half the story. The other half is where you stood and when. If you have ever written a location down on paper, or sent a photo followed by a separate map pin and still been asked "when was this?", PinCam is for you. Site engineers, surveyors, inspectors and travellers all need the same thing: the place and the time, right there on the picture. PinCam puts it all in one photo.

We care a lot about the camera itself. The shutter never waits for GPS, so you never miss a shot. The stamp you see on the viewfinder is exactly the stamp you get, drawn onto the full resolution photo, not a screenshot. Tap to focus, slide to brighten, switch between 0.5×, 1×, 2× and 4×, shoot in 4:3, 3:2, 16:9 or square, and line things up with a grid and a level that taps when it is straight.

The stamp is yours to shape. Turn each detail on or off, add a title like a project or site name, choose metric or imperial units, Celsius or Fahrenheit, and put the stamp on the photo or in a clean strip below it so nothing in the frame is covered. Name your files the way your team works, with the date, time, city, area or coordinates in the file name.

Lost signal on a construction site or a mountain road? PinCam keeps going. GPS works without mobile data, so the photo is still stamped with coordinates, date and time, and the gallery fills in the address the moment you are back online. The location is also saved inside the photo's EXIF data, so Google Photos, Apple Photos and mapping tools can place it on a map.

And it is private by design. There is no account to create. Your photos stay on your phone and go only where you choose to share them. PinCam uses your location only while the camera is open, never in the background.

How it works

Add location and time to photos in three steps.

  1. Open PinCam

    Allow the camera and location. The map, address and time appear live on the viewfinder.

  2. Pick your details

    Long press the stamp to choose what shows: map, address, coordinates, weather and more.

  3. Shoot and share

    The stamped photo saves to a PinCam album in your gallery, ready to send on WhatsApp, email or anywhere.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is PinCam?

PinCam is a GPS map camera for iPhone and Android. Every photo you take is stamped with a small map, the full address, GPS coordinates, the date and time, and, if you like, altitude, compass heading and the weather. Your photos are then sorted by city and area inside the app.

Is PinCam free?

Yes. Everything a location and time stamp needs is free. A small banner shows in the gallery, never on the camera. A few extras, like satellite maps and 48 MP photos, can be unlocked for 2 hours by watching a short video, or for good with PinCam Pro. Your own logo and name on the stamp come with PinCam Pro.

Does PinCam work without internet?

Yes. GPS works without a data connection, so offline photos are still stamped with coordinates, date and time. The map shows when its tiles are already cached, and the gallery looks up the address and files the photo by city as soon as you are back online.

Does PinCam save the location in the photo's EXIF data?

Yes. Besides the visible stamp, PinCam writes GPS latitude, longitude, altitude, the camera's direction and the time into the photo's EXIF metadata, so Google Photos, Apple Photos and mapping tools can place it on a map.

Can I choose what appears on the stamp?

Yes. Switch the map, address, coordinates, altitude, GPS accuracy, date and time, compass heading, temperature, wind and humidity on or off. Pick metric or imperial units, Celsius or Fahrenheit, decimal or degrees-minutes-seconds coordinates, and place the stamp on the photo or in a strip below it.

Can I add my company logo to the stamp?

Yes, with PinCam Pro. Add your own logo and name to the badge on every photo. It is handy for site reports, inspections and client work.

Does PinCam track my location in the background?

No. PinCam uses your location only while the camera is open on your screen. There is no account and your photos stay on your phone unless you choose to share them.
