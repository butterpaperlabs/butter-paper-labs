About this app

A GPS camera that remembers the place for you.

PinCam is a GPS camera for people who send photos as proof of their work. Every photo and video is stamped with where and when it was taken: the address, GPS coordinates, a small map, the date and time, and, if you want them, altitude, GPS accuracy, compass heading, the weather and a note of your own. Then it files the photo under the job site, city and area, so you can find it again in seconds.

We built it for contractors, inspectors, surveyors, installers and field teams, who all get asked the same two questions about a photo: where was this, and when? PinCam answers both on the picture itself, so a report, a client or a colleague never needs a separate map pin or a note on paper.

Save a job site once, with a pin on the map, a radius and its own address lines, and pick it when you arrive. Photos taken there carry the site's address lines and land in its own folder in the gallery. Add your company's name and logo to the stamp with PinCam Pro, and every photo looks like it came from you.

It is a proper camera, too. The shutter never waits for GPS, so you never miss a shot, and the stamp you see on the viewfinder is exactly the stamp you get. Tap to focus, slide to brighten, switch between 0.5×, 1×, 2× and 4×, shoot in 4:3, 3:2, 16:9 or square, line things up with a grid and a level, and switch to Video to get clips with the stamp burned in.

Lost signal on a site or a mountain road? PinCam keeps going. GPS works without mobile data, so the photo is still stamped with coordinates, date and time, and the gallery fills in the address the moment you are back online. The location is also saved inside the photo's EXIF data, so Google Photos, Apple Photos and mapping tools can place it on a map.

And it is private by design. There is no account to create. Your photos stay on your phone and go only where you choose to share them. PinCam uses your location only while the camera is open, never in the background. It speaks English, Hindi, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian, Korean, Vietnamese, Indonesian and Thai, stamp included.

How it works

Add location and time to photos in three steps.

  1. Open PinCam

    Allow the camera and location. The map, address and time appear live on the viewfinder.

  2. Set up your stamp

    Long press the stamp to choose what shows and add a note. Save your job site once, and pick it when you arrive.

  3. Shoot and share

    Stamped photos and videos save to a PinCam album and to the site’s folder in the app, ready to send on WhatsApp, email or anywhere.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is PinCam?

PinCam is a GPS camera for iPhone, with Android to follow. Every photo and video you take is stamped with the address, GPS coordinates, a small map, the date and time, and, if you like, altitude, GPS accuracy, compass heading, the weather and a note of your own. Photos are then sorted by saved job site, city and area inside the app.

Is PinCam free?

Yes. Everything a location and time stamp needs is free: the stamp, the note, one saved site, 8 MP photos and 1080p video. A small banner shows in the gallery, never on the camera. Quality extras such as satellite maps, 48 MP photos and 4K video can be unlocked for 2 hours by watching a short video, or for good with PinCam Pro. Your company name and logo and unlimited saved sites come with PinCam Pro: monthly, yearly with a 7-day free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Can I save my job sites?

Yes. Save a site once with a pin on the map, a radius and its own address lines, and pick it when you arrive. Photos taken there carry those address lines and are filed under the site's name. One saved site is free; PinCam Pro saves as many as you need.

Does PinCam stamp videos too?

Yes. Switch to Video and the stamp is burned into the clip, frozen at the moment you started recording, with the location also written into the file. 1080p is free; 1080p at 60 fps and 4K come with PinCam Pro.

Does PinCam work without internet?

Yes. GPS works without a data connection, so offline photos are still stamped with coordinates, date and time. The map shows when its tiles are already cached, and the gallery looks up the address and files the photo by city as soon as you are back online.

Does PinCam save the location in the photo's EXIF data?

Yes. Besides the visible stamp, PinCam writes GPS latitude, longitude, altitude, the camera's direction and the time into the photo's EXIF metadata, so Google Photos, Apple Photos and mapping tools can place it on a map.

Can I choose what appears on the stamp?

Yes. Switch the map, address, coordinates, altitude, GPS accuracy, date and time, compass heading and weather on or off, and add a one-line note such as a job number. Pick metric or imperial units, Celsius or Fahrenheit, decimal or degrees-minutes-seconds coordinates, and place the stamp on the photo or in a strip below it.

Can I add my company logo to the stamp?

Yes, with PinCam Pro. Add your company's name above the address and your own logo to the badge on every photo. It is made for site reports, inspections and client work.

Does PinCam track my location in the background?

No. PinCam uses your location only while the camera is open on your screen. There is no account and your photos stay on your phone unless you choose to share them.
